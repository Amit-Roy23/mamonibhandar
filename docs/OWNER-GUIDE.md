# MB Mart — Owner's Guide

Your online shop for **Maamoni Bhandar**, Mirza Bazaar, Udaipur. Customers within **4 km** of the shop can order, and they pay **Cash on Delivery**.

---

## 1. Logging in

1. Open your website and tap **Login** (top right). Or scroll to the bottom and tap **Owner / Admin login**.
2. Email: **maamonibhandar@gmail.com** · Password: the owner password you were given.
3. You will land on the **Admin panel**.

**Tip — make it an app on your phone:** open the website in Chrome → menu ⋮ → **Add to Home screen**. It opens like an app.

## 2. Every day

**Turn on new-order alerts (once per phone or computer):** in the admin menu, tap **Sound on** and **Alerts**, then allow notifications. While the admin panel is open you hear a chime and see a pop-up for every new order.

**Handle each order:** go to **Orders** and tap the order. Then:
1. **Mark as confirmed** — you accept it. You can call or WhatsApp the customer from the order page.
2. **Mark as packed** — the items are ready.
3. **Mark as out for delivery** — tap **Directions** to open Google Maps to the customer's exact pin.
4. **Mark as delivered** after you collect the cash. The order is marked *Paid* automatically.

Each step updates the customer's tracking page. If they gave an email, they also get an email.

**Can't fulfil an order?** Tap **Cancel order** and choose a reason. The stock goes back automatically.

**Need a paper copy?** Use **Print invoice** on the order page.

## 3. Products

- **Add:** Products → **Add product** → name, category, price, MRP and stock → **Upload** a photo → **Add to store**.
- **Several pack sizes** (e.g. 1 kg and 5 kg): tap **Sell in multiple pack sizes**. Each size gets its own price, MRP and stock.
- **Change stock quickly:** in the product list, tap the stock number, type the new amount and press Enter.
- **Stop selling temporarily:** turn off **Visible**. Customers can't see or order it.
- **Bestseller star ⭐:** shows the product in *Bestsellers* on the home page.
- Stock goes down by itself when customers order, and back up when an order is cancelled. The dashboard lists **Low stock** items.

## 4. Categories and home page

- **Categories:** add, rename or reorder them, and upload a real **square photo** for each (Categories → ✏️ → Upload).
- **Home banners:** the big slides and the two offer cards at the top of the home page. Change the photo, headline, button text and link, or turn them on and off. Changes show immediately.

## 5. Offers (coupons)

Coupons → **New coupon**. For example, `WELCOME50` gives ₹50 off orders above ₹299, once per customer. You can set an expiry date and a usage limit. Customers type the code at checkout.

## 6. Store settings

- **Accepting orders:** switch it off on holidays. Customers can browse but can't order.
- **Delivery fee, free delivery above, minimum order.**
- **Delivery area:** the shop pin and radius (4 km). To change it, paste the shop's Google Maps link and tap **Find**, or drag the pin.
- **Phone, WhatsApp, opening hours and the announcement bar** (the green strip at the top of the website).
- **System status:** green ticks mean everything works. Anything orange or red tells you what to fix, or who to ask.

## 7. Customers

- **Forgot password:** Customers → find the person → **Reset password**. Read the new password to them on the phone. They can change it later.
- **Block** someone who places fake orders. **Make admin** gives a staff member full access, so use it carefully.

## 8. Weekly backup (important)

Every week, download your data and keep it in Google Drive:
- **Orders → Download CSV** (choose dates, e.g. last week)
- **Products → CSV**
- **Customers → Download CSV**

The files open in Excel or Google Sheets. They're also handy for counting sales.

## 9. If something isn't working

- Check **Store settings → System status** first.
- Customer can't order? Ask whether their address is pinned and inside the green circle. They can tap **Check delivery** at the top of the website.
- Website down or showing errors? Contact your developer and share the error code shown on the page.
